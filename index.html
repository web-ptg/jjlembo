Hi, I’m John.

There are budgets to protect, people to lead, clients to serve, buildings to operate, projects to complete, and decisions to make with imperfect information. Somewhere between the theory and the daily reality is where the real work happens. That is where John has spent most of his career.

He has worked as an employee, executive, consultant, educator, mentor, and independent advisor, helping organizations ranging from startups and nonprofits to Fortune 500 companies make sense of complex operational and strategic challenges. His style is practical, direct, and grounded in experience. He is less interested in jargon and more interested in what works.

Whether advising a client, teaching students, mentoring professionals, or writing for readers, his focus is on turning confusion into understanding and understanding into useful action.

The book

So You Want to Be a Consultant

A clear-eyed, experience-driven guide to what consulting really is, beyond the title, the hype, or the misconceptions.

Written for professionals who are considering consulting, stepping into it, or trying to figure out what the profession really demands. Drawing on nearly four decades in the field, it covers the practical realities, hard lessons, and foundational principles that turn expertise into a trusted advisory practice: credibility, trust, value, client service, and building a practice that lasts.

“Consulting is a vocation that many enter into in the latter part of their career. It embodies the culmination of experiences and development of an expertise over time that allows one to offer their support as a professional advisor and obtain payment for services rendered.”

      The Navitas Group, LLC is a strategic facilities management consulting firm dedicated to optimizing building performance, strengthening operational infrastructure, and aligning facility operations with enterprise objectives.

      Founded in 2006, The Navitas Group was established to address a persistent challenge within the built environment: the disconnect between day-to-day building operations and executive-level strategy. Our work operates precisely at that intersection.

      Our practice is built on decades of senior leadership experience across healthcare, hospitality, commercial real estate, multi-building campuses, and complex institutional environments. We understand facilities not simply as brick-and-mortar infrastructure, but as mission-critical business assets that influence financial performance, risk exposure, and organizational stability.

      Where we’ve led

      • Hospitality
      • Commercial real estate
      • Multi-building campuses
      • Institutional environments

      What we do

      Advisory and implementation support across the full lifecycle of facility operations and asset management.

      • Engineering Operations & Maintenance Strategy
      • Facility Condition Assessments (FCA)
      • Capital Planning & Asset Lifecycle Modeling
      • Energy & Sustainability Master Planning and Program Implementation
      • Building Performance Optimization
      • Owner’s Representation & Project Oversight
      • FM Outsourcing vs. In-Sourcing Strategy Modeling
      • Strategic Sourcing and Vendor Alignment
      • Compliance & Risk Mitigation Frameworks
      • Technology, Analytics and Decision Support

      How we work

      Our methodology integrates multiple disciplines to produce clear, actionable results:

      • Technical analysis
      • Financial modeling and capital forecasting
      • Operational process design
      • Executive-level reporting and decision support

      Our philosophy

      We do not sell equipment.

      We do not represent manufacturers.

      We represent the owner’s interest.

      Our objective is measurable improvement in reliability, efficiency, compliance, and financial performance across the built environment. The Navitas Group serves as a trusted advisor to organizations that depend on complex facilities to support their mission, operations, and long-term enterprise value.
